The software licenses in this repository (see [NOTICE](NOTICE)) grant
rights in the code and the documentation. They grant no rights in the
project's name or its logo. That is deliberate: the license lets anyone
run, modify and distribute the software, while the name stays a reliable
way to tell where a build came from.

## What the name covers

The word **escapement** as the name of this project and of the service
operated by the project, and the escapement logo.

## Uses that need no permission

- Stating truthfully that your work relates to escapement: "a plugin for
  escapement", "compatible with escapement", "escapement fork".
- Referring to the project in articles, talks, documentation, courses and
  reviews.
- Keeping the name inside unmodified copies of the software you
  redistribute.

## Uses that need written permission

- Naming your own product, service, fork, distribution, company or
  domain "escapement", or a name likely to be confused with it.
- Using the logo as the icon or branding of your own product or service.
- Any use suggesting that the project endorses, sponsors or maintains
  your work.

If you distribute a modified version, please give it your own name and
say that it is derived from escapement. A modified build presented under
the project's name makes its bugs and its behaviour ours in the eyes of
users, which is the one thing this policy exists to prevent.
